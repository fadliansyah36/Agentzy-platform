## Nomination and acceptance procedure

1. Product Owner/Governance Owner supplies a named candidate and organization for each reviewer slot.
2. Verify competence using relevant experience/credentials and record evidence.
3. Obtain written independence declaration and conflict-of-interest statement.
4. Assess independence from implementation, control ownership and decisions being reviewed. Implementers cannot independently review their own remediation.
5. Send the matching scope in `PHASE_00_REVIEW_SCOPE_AND_INDEPENDENCE.md`.
6. Obtain explicit availability and acceptance.
7. Provide read-only evidence package and agreed schedule.
8. Record written findings, exceptions, recommendation, date and signed decision.

Allowed statuses: NOMINATED, PENDING CONFIRMATION, CONFIRMED, DECLINED, REPLACEMENT REQUIRED. A blank candidate with PENDING CONFIRMATION is not a completed nomination.
